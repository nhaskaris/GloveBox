Privacy Policy

Glovebox

Last updated: August 2026

Glovebox is built as a local-only application. This policy explains what that means for your data.

Local storage only

All data you enter into Glovebox — vehicle information, maintenance records, service history, and app settings — is stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded, synced, or sent anywhere.

No personal data collection

Glovebox does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers. There is no account to create and no analytics tracking your usage.

No third-party access

Because everything is stored locally, we have no ability to view, access, or share your data with any third party — there is simply nothing on our end to share.

Your control over your data

You own your data completely. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data from your device, permanently and immediately.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is ever updated, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date above.

By using Glovebox, you agree to this local-only data policy.
