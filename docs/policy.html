Privacy Policy

Glovebox is designed with your privacy in mind. All data entered into the application, including vehicle information, maintenance records, and settings, is stored locally on your device.

No Personal Data Collection: Glovebox does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal information to external servers.

Local Storage Only: All your data remains on your device. We do not have access to your data, and it is not shared with any third parties.

Your Control: You have full control over your data. If you delete the application, all locally stored data will be removed from your device.

By using Glovebox, you agree to this local-only data storage policy.
